# Habit Tracker

A phone-first personal planner in a single HTML file. Track **habits** (short term), **to-dos** (current) and **goals** (long term), with stats, a daily journal and achievements. No account, no build step, no server.

Repo: https://github.com/Ayanle5/habit

## Features

### Habits
- Two types: **done / not done**, or **counted** habits with a daily target, unit and per-tap step (e.g. 10,000 steps in 1,000 steps per tap). You can also tap the number and type an exact value.
- Flexible schedules: every day, specific days of the week, or a number of times per week.
- Current and best **streaks**. Weekly habits count streaks in weeks.
- **Skip days** (sick, travelling) without breaking a streak or hurting your stats.
- Per-habit page with a 17-week **heatmap**, 30-day completion rate and streaks.
- Step back through earlier days to fix a missed check-in.
- A **daily journal** at the bottom of the Today page, saved per day.

### To-do
- Each task has a day. Unfinished tasks **carry over** to today automatically.
- Schedule tasks for the future (Upcoming), including Tomorrow and Next week shortcuts.
- **Repeating** tasks: every day, week or month.
- Separate Today, Upcoming and Completed sections.

### Goals
- Long-term goals with an optional target date and **milestones**.
- Progress bar, days left or overdue, and an Achieved section.

### Stats
- Week or month completion chart, week-over-week change, best and toughest weekday.
- Check-ins, perfect days and top streak.
- Per-habit completion rates, to-do stats and goal progress.
- **Weekly review** screen and 15 **achievement badges** (streaks, check-ins, perfect days, goals, journal entries and more).

### Other
- Light and dark mode follow your phone's setting.
- Animations respect the reduced-motion setting.
- **Backup / restore** under Stats: copy your data as JSON and paste it back to restore.

## Getting started

The whole app is one file. Pick one of these:

1. **Open it locally.** Download the HTML file and open it in any modern browser.
2. **Host it with GitHub Pages.** Rename the file to `index.html`, then follow [GitHub's Pages guide](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site) to publish it from the repo.
3. **Install it on your phone.** Open the hosted page and use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option ([PWA basics](https://web.dev/learn/pwa)).
